Sieve of Eratosthenes (v3.0.3+0)

Architectural Design & Implementation Plan

1. Project Concept & Core Mechanics

The Sieve of Eratosthenes is an ancient, elegant algorithm for finding all prime numbers up to a given limit by iteratively marking the multiples of each prime as composite. This project turns the algorithm into a living, rhythmic, audio-visual experience and competitive reflex/number-sense game.

1.1 The Sieve Rhythm Engine

  • Beat Clock: The engine counts forward at an adjustable rate (default: 1.0 Hz=60 BPM1.0\text{ Hz} = 60\text{ BPM}).
  • State & Factorization:
    • Maintains an ordered set of discovered primes 𝒫={p1,p2,…}\mathcal{P} = \{p_1, p_2, \dots\}.
    • For each current integer N≥2N \ge 2:
      • Test NN against all known primes p∈𝒫p \in \mathcal{P} where p≤Np \le \sqrt{N} (or trial division).
      • If NN has prime factors, decompose NN into its prime factorization: N=p1a1⋅p2a2⋯pkakN = p_1^{a_1} \cdot p_2^{a_2} \cdots p_k^{a_k}
      • If NN is divisible by no known prime, NN is prime! NN is added to 𝒫\mathcal{P} and triggers a new prime arrival event.
  • Audio-Visual Emission on Beat:
    • For composite NN: Trigger audio tones for all prime factors pip_i, sounding simultaneously as a harmonic chord.
    • For prime NN: Introduce a distinct, prominent new sound and visual celebratory flare.
    • For prime powers (ai>1a_i > 1): Play the base prime note along with upper octaves (2×,4×2\times, 4\times) or higher velocity/gain per factor multiplicity.

2. Audio Design & Harmonic Piano Chords

2.1 Acoustic Grand Piano Samples

Rather than purely synthetic beeps, the app uses real acoustic piano samples to create rich, organic chords.

Open Source Piano Sample Libraries:

  • Tone.js Instruments / Soundfont Piano (MIT / CC0 License):
    • Lightweight, beautifully recorded acoustic grand piano samples.
    • Stored in compressed .mp3 or .ogg format with key note anchors across 4–5 octaves (e.g., C2C_2 to C6C_6).
    • Total asset size is under 1.5 MB1.5\text{ MB}, making it instantaneous to download on mobile web.
  • Salamander Grand Piano (CC-BY 3.0):
    • Captured from a Yamaha C5 grand piano. Pristine acoustic tone with natural hammer strike and room resonance.
  • Playback & Scaling Architecture:
    • Anchor samples (e.g., C2,E2,G2,C3,…C_2, E_2, G_2, C_3, \dots) are pre-decoded into memory buffers.
    • Intermediate pitches or higher primes are dynamically pitch-shifted using Web Audio buffer playback rate (playbackRate.value = targetFreq / baseFreq) or native audio resamplers, eliminating latency and memory bloat.

2.2 The Harmonic Prime Spectrum

In music theory and physics, the natural overtone series is built from integer multiples of a fundamental frequency. We map primes to musical intervals so that composite numbers produce natural, pleasant piano chords:

  • Prime 2 (The Octave / Root Fundamental):
    • Pitch: C3C_3 (130.81 Hz130.81\text{ Hz}).
    • Role: The rhythmic bass heartbeat and lowest acoustic anchor.
  • Prime 3 (The Perfect Fifth):
    • Pitch: G3G_3 (196.00 Hz196.00\text{ Hz}, 3/23/2 ratio).
    • Together with 2 (N=6N=6): Forms an open, resonant Power Chord (C−GC - G).
  • Prime 5 (The Major Third):
    • Pitch: E4E_4 (329.63 Hz329.63\text{ Hz}, 5/45/4 ratio).
    • Together with 2 & 3 (N=30=2⋅3⋅5N=30 = 2 \cdot 3 \cdot 5): Resolves into a bright, consonant C Major Triad (C−E−GC - E - G).
    • N=10=2⋅5N=10 = 2 \cdot 5: Major Tenth (C−EC - E).
    • N=15=3⋅5N=15 = 3 \cdot 5: Minor Third interval (E−GE - G).
  • Prime 7 (The Harmonic / Dominant 7th):
    • Pitch: B4♭B^\flat_4 (466.16 Hz466.16\text{ Hz}, 7/47/4 ratio).
    • Together with 2, 3, 5 (N=210N=210): Creates a rich Dominant 7th chord (C−E−G−B♭C - E - G - B^\flat).
  • Prime 11 (The Augmented 11th / Lydian Fourth):
    • Pitch: F5♯F^\sharp_5 (739.99 Hz739.99\text{ Hz}, 11/811/8 ratio).
  • Prime 13 (The Natural 13th / Major Sixth):
    • Pitch: A5A_5 (880.00 Hz880.00\text{ Hz}, 13/813/8 ratio).
  • Higher Primes (p≥17p \ge 17):
    • Mapped algorithmically into natural scale positions across the higher piano octaves (C5−C7C_5 - C_7) so the instrument continues sounding musical and coherent as high primes appear.

2.3 Factor Multiplicity (Higher Powers)

When a prime factor appears multiple times (N=pk,k≥2N = p^k, k \ge 2): * Octave Stacking: For each additional exponent power, add the corresponding octave harmonic (f⋅2k−1f \cdot 2^{k-1}). For instance: * 4=22⟹C3+C44 = 2^2 \implies C_3 + C_4 * 8=23⟹C3+C4+C58 = 2^3 \implies C_3 + C_4 + C_5 * 12=22⋅3⟹C3+C4+G312 = 2^2 \cdot 3 \implies C_3 + C_4 + G_3 * Velocity & Accent: Apply +2.0 dB+2.0\text{ dB} volume boost and slightly stronger acoustic strike per multiplicity.

3.2 Mathematical Verification of the Proximity Function

The plan proposed a progress bar metric: f(t)=|2⋅((speed⋅t)(mod⁡p))p−1.0|f(t) = \left| 2 \cdot \frac{((\text{speed} \cdot t) \pmod p)}{p} - 1.0 \right|

Continuity Proof & Analysis:

  1. Let x(t)=(speed⋅t)(mod⁡p)x(t) = (\text{speed} \cdot t) \pmod p, where x(t)∈[0,p)x(t) \in [0, p).
  2. Inside the open interval (kp,(k+1)p)(k p, (k+1) p), x(t)x(t) is linear and continuous.
  3. At the boundary where t→(kpspeed)−t \to \left(\frac{k p}{\text{speed}}\right)^-:
    • x(t)→p−x(t) \to p^-
    • 2x(t)p−1.0→2pp−1.0=+1.0\frac{2 x(t)}{p} - 1.0 \to \frac{2 p}{p} - 1.0 = +1.0
    • |+1.0|=1.0\left| +1.0 \right| = 1.0
  4. At the boundary where t=kpspeedt = \frac{k p}{\text{speed}} (from the right, t→+t \to^+):
    • x(t)=0x(t) = 0
    • 2(0)p−1.0=−1.0\frac{2(0)}{p} - 1.0 = -1.0
    • |−1.0|=1.0\left| -1.0 \right| = 1.0
  5. Conclusion: Because lim⁡t→t0−f(t)=lim⁡t→t0+f(t)=f(t0)=1.0\lim_{t \to t_0^-} f(t) = \lim_{t \to t_0^+} f(t) = f(t_0) = 1.0, the function is mathematically continuous for all t∈ℝt \in \mathbb{R}!
  6. Waveform Shape: It is an exact inverted triangle wave bounded between [0.0,1.0][0.0, 1.0]:
    • Value is 1.01.0 (Maximum bar height/width) exactly when speed⋅t\text{speed} \cdot t is a multiple of pp (N≡0(mod⁡p)N \equiv 0 \pmod p).
    • Value drops smoothly to 0.00.0 at the midpoint between multiples (N≡p/2(mod⁡p)N \equiv p/2 \pmod p).
    • It rises smoothly back to 1.01.0 as NN approaches the next multiple.

4. Single-Player Game Experience (Immediate Focus)

4.1 Zen / Exploration Mode

  • Tempo Slider: Smoothly adjust speed from 0.2 Hz0.2\text{ Hz} (1 beat per 5 seconds1\text{ beat per 5 seconds}) up to 10.0 Hz10.0\text{ Hz}.
  • Factor Inspector: Tap on any number on the board (past, present, or future) to inspect its prime factorization and hear its piano chord isolated.
  • Mute/Solo Prime Tracks: Toggle individual primes to isolate their rhythm and hear how the sieve weaves together.

4.2 Solo Reflex Challenge

  • The “PRIME!” Buzzer Button: A prominent button at the bottom of the screen.
  • Timing Window: The player must press the button when the next approaching number is prime (within a configurable reaction window before/at the beat).
  • Scoring Rules:
    • Tap on Prime: +100 points×speed multiplier+100 \text{ points} \times \text{speed multiplier}.
    • Tap on Composite: −75 points-75 \text{ points} penalty (visual screen shake and low thud sound).
    • Missed Prime: Streak resets to 0.
  • Streak Multiplier: Consecutive correct prime identifications build a combo multiplier (1.5×,2.0×,3.0×1.5\times, 2.0\times, 3.0\times).
  • Local High Scores: Stored locally in browser localStorage or device preferences.

5. Future Multiplayer Architecture & Cost Analysis

(To be implemented after the single-player experience is complete and reviewed)

5.1 Technology Selection

  • Wi-Fi Direct / P2P Wi-Fi: Android-only, requires invasive location/nearby permissions, breaks internet connectivity, and is completely unsupported on Web and iOS Safari.
  • Bluetooth (BLE): Does not support peripheral/hosting mode on Web browsers; requires native OS permissions.
  • Recommended: Firebase Realtime Database (Room Codes):
    • Instant room code join (e.g., room code 7351 or QR code).
    • Works on every platform (Android, iOS Safari, Chrome, Edge, desktop).
    • Sub-50ms synchronization with NTP server timestamp clock sync.

5.2 Cost Analysis (Zero-Cost Target)

  • Firebase Spark Plan (Free Tier):
    • Cost: $0.00 / month forever (No credit card required).
    • Realtime Database Limits:
      • 1 GB stored data (a room state is only ~2 KB).
      • 10 GB/month network download.
      • 100 simultaneous active client connections.
    • Firebase Hosting Limits:
      • 10 GB storage, 360 MB/day data transfer, free SSL on custom domain.
    • Conclusion: The free Spark tier easily supports thousands of active game sessions per month at zero cost.
