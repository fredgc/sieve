Privacy Policy for Sieve of Eratosthenes

Last Updated: October 31, 2026

Zero-PII & Privacy by Design

The Sieve of Eratosthenes (“the App”) is an interactive educational math game and rhythm demonstration. We believe in strict Privacy by Design: we do not collect personal information, we do not require user accounts, we do not track you across the web, and we do not sell or monetize your data.

This App is built for students, educators, and math/music enthusiasts of all ages.

Information We Do NOT Collect

To maintain a zero-PII (Personal Identifiable Information) architecture:

  • No User Accounts: We never ask for or store your name, email address, phone number, or password.

  • No Device Sensors or Permissions: We do not access your device location (GPS), camera, microphone, photo library, contacts, or local files.

  • No Third-Party Trackers or Advertisements: The App is 100% ad-free and contains no advertising SDKs, marketing trackers, or cross-site tracking cookies (no Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, etc.).

Information Handled During Gameplay

Single-Player Mode (100% On-Device)

  • All single-player statistics—including all-time high scores, longest prime streaks, guess accuracy, tempo settings, and audio preferences—are saved exclusively in your browser’s local storage (localStorage / device SharedPreferences).

  • This data remains strictly on your device and is never uploaded to any server.

Multiplayer Mode (Ephemeral / Self-Destructing)

When you create or join a multiplayer room:

  • Screen Name: You may provide a temporary nickname (e.g., “Alice” or “Player 1”) to identify yourself to other players in the room.

  • Gameplay State: Live scores, streaks, prime prediction guesses, and the 4-character room code are transmitted over an encrypted WebSocket connection solely to synchronize the match in real time.

  • Automatic Deletion: Multiplayer room data is strictly temporary. The entire room and all player entries are automatically deleted immediately upon match completion or after 10 minutes of inactivity.

Third-Party Infrastructure

The App uses Google Firebase (Google LLC) for static web hosting and real-time multiplayer message synchronization:

  • Google processes standard technical connection metadata (such as IP addresses and network routing headers) as necessary to establish encrypted WebSocket connections and protect against denial-of-service attacks.

  • For more information on Google’s privacy practices, refer to Google’s Privacy Policy .

Children & Classroom Safe (COPPA & FERPA)

The App is designed to be fully safe for children and classroom environments:

  • We comply with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA).

  • Because the App does not request, collect, or store any personal information from users of any age, it does not require parental consent and can be safely deployed on school devices and in educational settings.
