The **Sieve of Eratosthenes** is an interactive educational math game and
rhythm demonstration designed to transform number theory and prime discovery
into an interactive audio-visual experience.

## History & Motivation

Created in the 3rd century BCE by Greek mathematician and chief librarian of
Alexandria **Eratosthenes of Cyrene** (c. 276–c. 195 BCE), the Sieve is one of
the most intuitive algorithms in mathematics for finding all prime numbers.

For historical context and algorithmic theory, read the
[Wikipedia article on the Sieve of Eratosthenes][wiki].

## Audio-Visual Analogy

To explore how prime numbers are assigned atomic overtone notes and composite
numbers create musical chords, see the [Audio-Visual Analogy](analogy.html).

This application is written in Dart with Flutter—the source code is available
on GitHub at [https://github.com/fredgc/sieve][github].

## Privacy and Data Retention

This program allows you to customize settings that are saved locally on your
device (using SharedPreferences / LocalStorage). No personal data is
collected.

On Android, local settings are deleted when you uninstall the app. On Web,
settings are deleted when you clear your browser's local application data.
